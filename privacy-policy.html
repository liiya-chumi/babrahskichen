Privacy policy

How Barbrahs Kitchen collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Barbrahs Kitchen (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and when you contact us, why we collect it, and your rights under the South African Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”).

1. Who we are

The responsible party for your personal information is Barbrahs Kitchen, Visagie Street, Kortze and M3 Street. You can reach us on 071 282 3848 or at info@barbrahskitchen.co.za.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, phone number, email address (optional), the topic and the message you write, and a record that you agreed to this policy.
  • Phone and WhatsApp: if you call or message us to order, we see your phone number, WhatsApp profile name and the content of your messages.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Embedded map: only if you choose to load it, Google receives your IP address and browser details (see section 5).

We do not ask for payment card details on this website and we do not knowingly collect special personal information.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and prepare, confirm and hand over your orders;
  • To arrange catering or special events you ask about;
  • To keep the website secure and working properly;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We rely on your consent (when you submit the form or accept optional cookies), on taking steps at your request to enter into a contract (orders), and on our legitimate interest in running a safe, working business. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for marketing unless you separately ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

We only share information with service providers that help us run the website and respond to you, and only as needed:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) – processes contact form submissions and forwards them to our email inbox.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) – if you choose to message us there, under WhatsApp’s own privacy terms.
  • Google Maps – only if you accept optional cookies or click “Load map”.
  • Our website host and email provider.

Some of these providers are located outside South Africa, so your information may be transferred across borders. We take reasonable steps to make sure such providers protect it in a way comparable to POPIA. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. Cookies & local storage

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all or only essential storage. You can change your choice any time using “Cookie settings” in the page footer.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and order messages only as long as needed to deal with them and for a reasonable period afterwards (generally no more than 12 months), unless the law requires us to keep something longer. After that we delete or anonymise it.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections where available. No online service is completely risk-free, so please do not send sensitive information through the contact form.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your personal information and give you a copy;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete or destroy it where we no longer need it;
  • stop processing it or object to its processing; and
  • withdraw consent you have given (this does not affect processing already done).

To exercise these rights, email info@barbrahskitchen.co.za. We may need to verify your identity first.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children under 18. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.

11. Contact & complaints

Questions about this policy? Contact us at info@barbrahskitchen.co.za or 071 282 3848. If you believe we have not handled your information properly you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.
